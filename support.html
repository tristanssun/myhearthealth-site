Scanning a handwritten log

Tap Bulk Import From Photo, then take a photo or choose one. The camera finds the page edges for you, and you can add several pages. In a few seconds you'll see every reading the app found. Check them, fix anything that looks wrong, untick any you don't want, then save.

Tips: fill the frame with the page, avoid glare and shadows, and keep the writing in focus. Sideways or upside-down photos are fine. If a value looks wrong, tap it to edit before saving.

Logging from your monitor

Tap Log Blood Pressure and choose Take Photo to photograph the monitor's display, or Manual Entry to type the numbers. Photo readings always open for review before they're saved.

Sharing with your doctor

Open Browse Readings and tap the share button to send a PDF report (with charts and a full table) or a CSV spreadsheet by email, Messages, AirDrop or Files.

Profiles and deleting data

Use the name at the top of the screen to switch between, add, rename or delete profiles. Swipe a reading in Browse Readings to delete it. Clear All Data removes every reading for the current profile. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device.

Privacy

Readings stay on your device. Photos you choose to scan are sent to Anthropic's Claude API only to read the numbers. There are no accounts, ads or tracking. See the privacy policy.

MyHeartHealth is a record-keeping tool, not a medical device. It does not diagnose or treat any condition.
