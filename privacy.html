MyHeartHealth Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

MyHeartHealth helps you keep a record of your blood pressure and pulse. This policy explains, in plain language, what happens to your information when you use the app.

The short version

  • There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Your readings are stored only on your device.
  • When you choose to scan a photo, that photo is sent to Anthropic's Claude API so the app can read the numbers. Nothing else is sent, and no other service receives it.
  • We never sell your information or share it for advertising.

Information stored on your device

The app stores the profiles you create (just a name you choose, such as "Mum" or "Patient 1") and the readings you save: date, optional time, systolic and diastolic pressure, and optional pulse. This information lives in the app's private storage on your iPhone or iPad. We (the developer) do not have access to it.

It is included in your device's own backups (for example iCloud or computer backups) according to your device settings, like other app data.

You can delete individual readings, a whole profile, or all readings for a profile at any time from within the app. Deleting the app removes all of its data from the device.

Photos and the camera

The app asks for camera or photo access only when you choose to scan something, and only uses the photo you take or pick. Photos are not saved by the app.

Information sent off your device

Reading numbers from a photo needs help from an online service. Information is only sent when you start one of these scans:

What you doWhat is sentSent toWhy
Scan a photo of your blood pressure monitorThe photoAnthropic (Claude API)To read the systolic, diastolic and pulse numbers from the display
Scan or import a handwritten log (one or more pages)The photos of the pagesAnthropic (Claude API)To read the dated readings written on the page

Before sending, the app resizes each photo to a standard size and adds three rotated copies of it, so sideways or upside-down photos can still be read. Anthropic is the only service that receives your photos.

Readings you type in by hand, your saved readings, and your profile names are not sent anywhere.

The data is sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection and is not linked to your name, an account, an email address or any device identifier we control. The results come straight back to the app, where you review them before anything is saved.

Anthropic processes the photos on our behalf to deliver the feature. Under its policies for API customers, Anthropic automatically deletes API inputs and outputs within 30 days (longer only in limited cases such as enforcing its usage policies or meeting legal requirements), and it does not use them to train its models by default. You can read its policies:

  • Anthropic: https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy and https://www.anthropic.com/legal/commercial-terms

If you would rather not send photos anywhere, you can enter every reading manually; all other features work without an internet connection.

Exporting and sharing

If you export your readings as a CSV file or PDF report, the app creates the file and opens the standard iOS share sheet. Where it goes next (email, Messages, Files, your doctor's portal, etc.) is your choice and is handled by the app you pick.

Children

MyHeartHealth is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Medical disclaimer

MyHeartHealth is a record-keeping tool. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose or treat any condition. Talk to a healthcare professional about your readings.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we will update this policy and the "Last updated" date, and describe significant changes in the app's release notes.
